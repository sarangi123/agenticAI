FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Q: What are your business hours?
A: We are open Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (EST). We are closed on weekends and public holidays.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address and follow the link we send you. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not accept cash or checks.

Q: What is your refund policy?
A: You can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: You can reach customer support by email at support@example.com or by phone at 1-800-555-0199 during business hours.

Q: Do you offer international shipping?
A: Yes, we ship to over 50 countries. International shipping times range from 7 to 21 business days depending on the destination.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. You can enter that number on our website's "Track Order" page.

Q: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
A: Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be modified.
